## Rules for this register

Record publisher, title, URL, publication or effective date, access date, and the
relevant section. Distinguish a legal requirement from a regulator
recommendation, a platform policy, a lender overlay, an industry norm, and an
opinion. Record conflicts between sources rather than silently choosing one.
Never cite a search-result summary when the official source can be read. Never
present an uploaded PDF as controlling law without checking its date and status.
